## Section 3.5. Second-Order Effects

**Competitive response:** New copper supply is slow to permit and build, which supports long-term pricing. But high prices can still incentivize brownfield expansions, secondary supply, substitution, and efficiency.

**Reflexivity:** High copper prices improve cash flow, support project sanctioning, and can lower perceived balance-sheet risk. The negative reflexive loop is also real: if copper falls, FCF drops quickly, project economics weaken, and investors can re-rate the stock as a cyclical miner again.

**Customer behavior:** Copper demand is linked to grid investment, EVs, AI/data-center power, industrial electrification, China, and construction. The secular demand case is strong, but demand is not immune to recession or China weakness.

**Network dynamics:** No network effect. FCX’s moat is physical scarcity, permitting barriers, geology, mine engineering, and operating know-how.

## Section 7. Risks & Catalysts

### Top 3 Risks

1. **Copper price downside:** FCX has very high sensitivity to copper; 2026 OCF changes by about **$220M** for each **$0.10/lb** copper move for the rest of the year. ([Q4cdn][2])
2. **Grasberg ramp risk:** FCX now expects Grasberg Block Cave production to be about **65% of capacity** in 2H 2026, **80% by mid-2027**, and approach full capacity by end-2027, lower than prior expectations. ([Q4cdn][2])
3. **Political / social / regulatory risk:** FCX explicitly highlights political/social risks in Indonesia, Peru, relations with local communities and Indigenous Peoples, operational underground-mining risk, and mine-plan delays. ([Q4cdn][2])

### Top 3 Catalysts

1. Grasberg ramp progresses faster than the revised plan.
2. Copper remains above **$5.50–6.00/lb** while capex stays controlled.
3. Bagdad, Safford/Lone Star, El Abra, leaching initiatives, and Kucing Liar add credible long-term production growth. FCX says El Abra could add over **700M lb/year** of copper production, and Kucing Liar reserves are estimated around **8B lb copper** and **8M oz gold**, with initial production expected around 2030. ([Q4cdn][2])

**Highest-impact bear scenario:** Copper falls while Grasberg remains delayed and FCX continues spending heavily on growth projects, compressing FCF and forcing the stock to trade like a high-capex cyclical miner again.
