Section 3.5. Second-Order Effects

Competitive response:
Amazon AWS and Google Cloud will likely compete aggressively on AI workloads; expected impact -1/3. Oracle may compete on cloud infrastructure price/performance; expected impact -0.5/3. Open-source AI and model commoditization may pressure Copilot pricing; expected impact -1/3.

Reflexivity:
Positive reflexivity exists because a high stock price supports SBC, talent acquisition, and M&A currency. Negative reflexivity is moderate because even after a drawdown, Microsoft retains net cash and strong FCF.

Customer evolution:
Enterprise AI adoption may move from early adopters to broader seat expansion. The risk is that Copilot attach rates plateau or ROI is harder to prove outside developers and power users.

Network dynamics:
Microsoft benefits from enterprise workflow lock-in across Office, Teams, Azure AD, GitHub, Dynamics, and security. Winner-takes-most is plausible in enterprise productivity, but cloud infrastructure remains multi-cloud.
