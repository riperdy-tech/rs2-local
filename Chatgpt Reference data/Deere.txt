## Section 2.5. Adjusted Financials

For Deere, I normalize earnings rather than use raw TTM P/E. TTM EPS is about **$17.65**, calculated as FY25 EPS **$18.50** plus H1 FY26 EPS **$8.97** minus H1 FY25 EPS **$9.82**. Deere’s reported finance feed EPS is essentially the same at **$17.64**. 

FY25 consolidated operating cash flow was **$7.459B**, down from **$9.231B** in FY24, and FY25 repurchases were **$1.138B** versus **$4.007B** in FY24, showing Deere is pulling back buybacks in a weaker cycle. 

**Adjusted financials judgment:** Deere’s current earnings are likely below mid-cycle, but the stock price already anticipates recovery.

## Section 3.5. Second-Order Effects

**Competitive response:** CNH, AGCO, Kubota, Caterpillar, Komatsu, and regional OEMs compete across segments. Deere’s strongest advantage is in North American large ag and integrated precision-ag workflows; competition is tougher in smaller equipment and construction.

**Reflexivity:** A high stock price supports buybacks and confidence, but buybacks at high-cycle-recovery multiples are less accretive. Deere reduced repurchases materially in FY25, which is prudent but lowers EPS compounding. 

**Customer behavior:** Farmers can defer equipment purchases for years by using older machines, rental, repair, or used equipment when crop prices and margins are weak. This makes the timing of the recovery uncertain.

## Section 7. Risks & Catalysts

### Top 3 Risks

1. **Large-ag cycle lasts longer than expected.** Deere still expects U.S./Canada Large Ag down **15–20%** and Production & Precision Ag sales down **5–10%** in FY26. ([PR Newswire][1])
2. **Margins disappoint.** Production & Precision Ag operating margin fell to **15.7%** in Q2 from **22.0%** a year earlier. ([PR Newswire][1])
3. **High valuation on trough earnings.** The stock’s **35.8x** P/E requires confidence that trough earnings normalize.

### Top 3 Catalysts

1. FY26 proves to be the bottom of the large-ag cycle.
2. Production & Precision Ag margins stabilize and begin recovering.
3. Construction & Forestry and Small Ag continue offsetting large-ag weakness.

**Highest-impact bear scenario:** Large-ag remains weak into 2027 while the stock derates from “recovery multiple” back toward a normal industrial cyclical multiple.
