## Stage 0: offline, no GPU
**0.1 H3 decomposition (amendment D).**
- **All 22 pilot runs, not 3,** are recomputed by independent code, and each saved Base value is reproduced to the cent with every
  difference explained.
- **Two outputs:**
  1. **mechanical:** the contribution of years 1–2, years 3–5, the fade and the terminal value, and the sensitivity to years 1–2 with the
     later inputs held fixed;
  2. **reconciled counterfactuals:** alternative near-term paths propagated through the normalization and margin rules by explicit,
     written rules.
- **The counterfactuals are defined before computing:**
  - "consensus years 1–2";
  - "the analyst's stated near-term and normalized figures", by an exact formula for the year-1 and year-2 values;
  - "the company's own trend", with **through-cycle and trailing both reported**, because a trailing trend misleads at a cyclical peak.

**0.2 The normalization bridge, one canonical mathematical path (amendment A).**
- **A written spec** defines:
  - **normalized earnings:** the metric, the accounting basis, and "today's revenue scale";
  - **the normalization horizon:** the year the abnormal margin condition ends;
  - **structural growth:** revenue growth, a defined metric;
  - **the forecast path:** how revenue, margin, working capital and cash flow relate. It states exactly how it replaces or wraps the desk's
    current earnings-based path, in which revenue is implied from earnings at the year-5 margin.
  - **the consistency checks:** which derived quantity is compared with which declared one, at which horizon. **The primary check is the
    margin path against the declared normalized margin and horizon,** not absolute earnings.
- **Independent assumptions are kept apart from derived values,** and the checks are deterministic code, never a second model reading prose.
- **`forecast_type` is replaced by composable flags** (cyclical, structural growth, structural decline, turnaround), since a company can be
  cyclical and a structural grower at once.

**0.3 The forecast contract on the desk.**
- The fields come from 0.2.
- A mismatch draws a structured reason request in the same turn. Left unresolved, the run is not usable.
- Nothing is clamped or overwritten.

**0.4 Thresholds (amendment C).** In this order:
1. the semantics (0.2);
2. label the pilot paths under the revised rubric, kept apart from the blind scores;
3. **synthetic trajectories** with known good, bad and boundary properties;
4. calibrate the per-metric, per-horizon thresholds on a designated calibration set;
5. **freeze and register them before Stage 1;**
6. judge generalization only on cases not used to choose them.

MU's four runs count as one company, not four examples. **The Stage 2 names are sealed now and untouched until everything is frozen.**

**0.5 Classification checks.** Each checks its own variable:
- the growth trajectory against the change in growth rates;
- the cycle against the cyclical margin measure ("peak" does not force negative growth);
- margin persistence against the long-run margin compared with the current one;
- "shrinking" against positive growth.

They return reason requests and **never change a label.**

**0.6 The two-pass protocol** (for both arms B and C; see Stage 1). The analyst records a preliminary forecast, then reconciles it, with a
change log on the verdict row.

**0.7 Memo retention.** A deterministic merge after a crux follow-up; never "unchanged from prior".

**0.8 Masking.** Every label word (including "Strong Buy"), MoS columns, momentum percentages and direction phrases, with
`blind_traj.py` made part of the tooling. **Test:** 0 residuals on all 22 pilot memos.

**0.9 Crux reconciliation (refinement).** Report **candidate citations** separately from **the final citation that supports each
directional verdict.** That resolves "26–30% unresolved" against "every directional call had a verified crux".

**0.10 Versioning (refinement).** Separate version identifiers, each stamped on every run, and the full configuration frozen for
confirmation:
- the pack (`PACK_REVISION` 25);
- the desk schema;
- the runner protocol;
- the evaluation rubric.

**Not changed:** the model, the server pins, the sampling, the context, the evidence, the tool budgets, the gates, the RONIC and
working-capital fixes, and P50 (omitted).

## v4 additions (course check gate 1, ruled by the coordinator, 2026-10-16 23:55)
- **"Unresolved" is defined, before Stage 1.**
  - **Unresolved:** a C or K check that is `fail` on the final desk run **without** `reason_given`.
  - A `fail` with `reason_given` is **resolved** by the desk's rule. Whether the reason is any good is judged only by the blind rubric,
    never by counting fails.
  - The expansion criterion "no unresolved normalization or classification contradiction" reads this definition.
- **Failure classes are model outcomes, not runner defects.** The following count as the run's result and are never fixed and re-run
  inside Stage 1:
  - a reason loop;
  - an exhausted budget;
  - an unresolved fail;
  - a two-pass breach;
  - a lost memo;
  - a refused contract.

  **Only these are defects:** a crash or exception in the runner or desk code; a server or pin failure; a frozen artefact that is missing
  or changed. Each defect is recorded, and only the affected run is re-run, with the same seed.
- **The Muse paper book is a build step, in parallel with Stage 1's GPU time:**
  - the name `rn_muse`, paper only, never `rn_depth`;
  - its own overlay and publication path, modelled on the Gemini interim arm;
  - a TRK-06 entry.

  It is ready before Stage 1 is scored, so a pass can start it at once.
- **The rubric** is written, frozen and hashed into the Stage 1 registration before any Stage 1 run.
- **Corrected:** "Not changed: … the working-capital fixes" is wrong for the r25 arms. The bridge replaces step 2d's base-normalized
  working capital with intensity × ΔR on the declared revenue (spec §4), by design. Arm A keeps step 2d.
