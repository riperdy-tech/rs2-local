## 5. Delisting/census three-bound gate — INVALID (data contract)
Original Baseline A `census` treatment adds pseudo-identifiers `CIK##########` for departed companies with **synthetic missing-return rules**, not observed trading-price paths. A1's quarterly-held book may continue owning one when the monthly S4 selection row disappears. No independent adjusted entry price exists for that pseudo-name. Unlike monthly A0's conditional selection, a quarterly holding requires a specified, auditable *month-by-month terminal event timing and state transition*.

- `census / excluded`: first month 2017-01 has unscorable selected `CIK0001580608` (the original exclusion bound treats its return as missing).
- `census / minus100`: stops at **2017-07**, held `CIK0001062613` missing from S4 and without an observed price.
- `census / last_close`: stops at **2017-07** on the same pseudo-name.
- Inventing a flat return, using present-day quotes or dropping the survivor at the end of the month would induce selection/look-ahead bias. Therefore **no three-bound A1 performance comparison is valid**. The program reports `INVALID` instead of publishing incomplete metrics.
