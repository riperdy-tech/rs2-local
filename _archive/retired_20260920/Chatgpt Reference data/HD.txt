**T-1:** latest reported financials: fiscal Q1 2026. Home Depot reported Q1 sales of **$41.8B**, up **4.8%**, comparable sales up **0.6%**, U.S. comparable sales up **0.4%**, net earnings of **$3.3B**, and diluted EPS of **$3.30**. ([홈디포 투자자 관계][1])

**Archetype:** **A/B hybrid — Stable Incumbent + Quality Compounder, housing-cycle sensitive**
**Valuation Engine:** **Engine 1 — Traditional DCF + Multiples**

**Why this engine:**

1. HD is a mature cash-generative retailer with strong brand, scale, supplier power, and high ROIC.
2. It is not a high-beta option-led stock; most value comes from existing cash flows, margins, and capital returns.
3. It has cyclical housing exposure, but not enough cyclicality to require a pure Engine 2 cycle valuation.

**Model Confidence:** **Medium-High.** Financial disclosure is strong; the largest uncertainty is macro-driven timing of housing turnover and big-ticket renovation recovery.

## Section 3.5. Second-Order Effects

**Competitive response:**
Lowe’s remains the direct retail competitor, while Amazon and specialty distributors pressure certain categories. HD’s Pro ecosystem, vendor scale, store network, and supply-chain depth reduce competitive risk, but price competition can increase if housing demand stays weak.

**Reflexivity:**
Positive: a stable stock supports low-cost capital access and investor tolerance during housing-cycle weakness.
Negative: if the market sees HD as over-levered after acquisitions and without buybacks, the stock can derate despite stable operations.

**Customer behavior evolution:**
Consumers are still doing smaller projects, but large discretionary renovations remain pressured by affordability and financing costs. Q1 comparable sales were helped by a **2.2%** average-ticket increase but hurt by a **1.3%** decline in comparable customer transactions, which is not a fully healthy demand mix. ([홈디포 투자자 관계][1])

**Network dynamics:**
The Pro strategy can widen HD’s moat if cross-selling, jobsite delivery, and specialty trade distribution create higher retention and wallet share.

## Section 7. Risks & Catalysts

### Top 3 Risks

1. **Housing-cycle drag:** high affordability pressure keeps big-ticket renovation demand weak.
2. **Margin pressure:** Q1 gross margin fell to **33.0%** from **33.8%**, partly due GMS inclusion and mix. ([홈디포 투자자 관계][3])
3. **Debt / capital return delay:** buybacks are paused, and management does not plan to resume repurchases in FY26 while reducing debt. ([홈디포 투자자 관계][3])

### Top 3 Catalysts

1. Comparable sales move toward the high end of the **flat to +2%** FY26 guide.
2. Operating margin stabilizes within the **12.4–12.6% GAAP** or **12.8–13.0% adjusted** guidance range. ([홈디포 투자자 관계][1])
3. Housing turnover improves, allowing big-ticket and Pro demand to recover.

**Highest-impact bear scenario:** Housing demand remains weak while acquisition-related debt and margin pressure delay buybacks, forcing a P/E derating.
