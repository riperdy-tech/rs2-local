## SECTION 0. Analysis Overview

Medtronic is a diversified global medtech company with improving growth after years of underperformance. Fiscal Q4 2026 revenue was $9.807 billion, up 9.9% reported and 6.6% organic, ahead of implied guidance [Actual]. Q4 GAAP diluted EPS was $0.96 and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $1.55 [Actual]. Full-year fiscal 2026 revenue was $36.364 billion, up 8.4% reported and 5.8% organic, the highest annual revenue growth in 10 years, while full-year non-GAAP diluted EPS was $5.53 [Actual].

Cardiac Ablation Solutions was a standout, with revenue up 78% globally and 124% in the U.S., gaining 8 points of U.S. share [Actual]. At $83.19, MDT has a $107.27 billion market cap, P/E of 23.24x, and EPS of $3.58 [Actual]. On FY2026 non-GAAP EPS of $5.53, the multiple is about 15.0x [Estimate].

Core view: Buy. MDT is not a hypergrowth name, but improving organic growth plus a reasonable valuation creates attractive risk/reward.

## SECTION 1. Macro Context

Medtech demand is relatively defensive because procedures are medically necessary, but hospital capital budgets, staffing, procedure volumes, FX, and reimbursement matter. Medtronic is global, so currency can affect reported results.

Rates matter less than for speculative growth because MDT is profitable and dividend-paying. The bigger macro issue is healthcare utilization and pricing/reimbursement.

Macro conclusion: defensive growth with procedure-volume sensitivity.

## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

Large-cap medtech companies with mid-single-digit organic growth and strong pipelines can generate solid returns when bought at mid-teens earnings multiples. The base rate improves when growth is accelerating after a stale period.

Base-rate score: positive.
