## SECTION 1. Macro Context

Broadcom is tied to AI data-center capex, hyperscaler spending, networking buildouts, enterprise software budgets, and semiconductor cycles. AI capex remains a massive tailwind. If hyperscalers continue building custom accelerators and Ethernet networking, Broadcom is a prime beneficiary.

Macro risk is concentration in AI spending expectations. If AI capex pauses or hyperscaler budgets normalize, the stock can de-rate even if results remain strong. Rates also matter because high-multiple AI infrastructure stocks are sensitive to discount-rate changes.

Macro conclusion: powerful secular tailwind, high expectation risk.

## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

The base rate for dominant semiconductor platforms is strong when they control essential designs, customer-specific silicon, and networking standards. Broadcom also has software cash flows, improving durability.

The base-rate warning is that semiconductor cycles are brutal and AI expectations can overshoot. Q2 AI revenue growth of 143% is exceptional [Actual], but such growth cannot be extrapolated indefinitely.

Base-rate score: very positive business, valuation-sensitive stock.

## SECTION 3.5. Second-Order Effects

AI custom silicon creates second-order lock-in. Once hyperscalers design around Broadcom ASIC and networking platforms, revenue can become multi-year and sticky. Networking bottlenecks in AI clusters also reinforce Broadcom's role.

The negative second-order effect is customer concentration. A few hyperscalers can dominate AI revenue, creating lumpy demand and bargaining power.

## SECTION 4. Scenarios

Base case: AI revenue keeps growing strongly, software integrates well, and Broadcom compounds earnings. Current price is reasonable but not cheap [Estimate].

Bull case: custom accelerators and AI networking become a multi-year supercycle, supporting further upside despite premium valuation [Estimate].

Probability-weighted view: excellent hold, disciplined new buy.
