## SECTION 0. Analysis Overview

Theravance Biopharma is a small biopharma company centered on YUPELRI economics through its Viatris collaboration and shareholder-value focus. Q1 2026 total revenue was $17.7 million, consisting entirely of Viatris collaboration revenue, up 15% year over year [Actual]. Theravance's implied 35% share of YUPELRI net sales was $21.9 million, up 7% [Actual]. R&D expense declined to $5.8 million from $11.5 million due to restructuring and CYPRESS wind-down [Actual].

At $16.93, TBPH has a market cap of $868.2 million, P/E of 7.59x, and EPS of $2.23 [Actual]. The low multiple reflects limited growth, small-cap biotech history, and questions about sustainable cash flows.

Core view: Speculative Hold. TBPH is cheap on headline earnings, but RS2 needs a cleaner long-term cash-flow bridge before upgrading.

## SECTION 1. Macro Context

## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

The base rate for small biopharma cash-flow stories is mixed. If management returns capital and costs are controlled, shareholders can do well. If cash flow is consumed by low-probability R&D, value leaks.

TBPH's restructuring and lower R&D expense improve the base rate [Actual]. The warning is that a single collaboration-linked product stream does not deserve a high multiple without growth or durable capital returns.

Base-rate score: neutral.

## SECTION 2. Business Quality

YUPELRI is an FDA-approved revefenacin inhalation solution for maintenance treatment of COPD [Actual]. Theravance does not record all product sales directly because Viatris records total net sales; Theravance receives collaboration economics [Actual].

This structure can be cash-efficient, but it limits control. TBPH's quality depends on Viatris execution, COPD market durability, cost discipline, and management's shareholder-value decisions.

Quality score: 5.5/10. Cash-flow potential, but limited control and growth.

The negative second-order effect is limited control: if Viatris execution weakens or COPD competitive dynamics shift, Theravance has less direct ability to fix the revenue trajectory.
