## SECTION 0. Analysis Overview

Red Violet is a data and analytics company focused on identity intelligence, fraud/risk solutions, investigative tools, and its FOREWARN real-estate safety product. The business has attractive software/data economics: high adjusted gross margins, high adjusted EBITDA margins, no debt, and operating leverage.

Q1 2026 was strong. Revenue increased 17% year over year to $25.8M [Actual]. Gross profit increased 22% to $19.3M [Actual], with gross margin improving to 75% [Actual]. Adjusted gross profit increased 20% to $22.0M [Actual], with adjusted gross margin of 85% [Actual]. Net income increased 28% to $4.4M [Actual], or $0.30 diluted EPS [Actual]. Adjusted EBITDA increased 27% to $10.7M [Actual], with a 41% adjusted EBITDA margin [Actual]. Operating cash flow was $6.6M [Actual], and free cash flow was $3.1M [Actual/Estimate]. Cash was $43.5M [Actual], with no debt [Actual].

Customer metrics are also positive. StockTitan's Q1 summary reports +400 IDI customers and +27,662 FOREWARN users [Actual/Estimate from company filing summary]. Management commentary suggested Q1 2025 included $1.2M of one-time revenue, so underlying Q1 2026 growth would have exceeded 20% on a comparable basis [Actual/Estimate].

The valuation is the restraint. At about $65.49 and market cap around $923M-$924M [Actual], RDVT trades around 52x Q1 annualized diluted EPS [Estimate], about 21.6x Q1 annualized adjusted EBITDA [Estimate], and about 9.0x Q1 annualized revenue [Estimate], before adjusting for cash. That is a premium valuation for a company growing revenue in the high teens.

RS2 conclusion: RDVT is a Hold / buy-on-pullback. The business is attractive and may compound well, but the current price already discounts high-quality execution. A better entry is below $55, or after several quarters of 20%+ underlying growth with sustained high-30s adjusted EBITDA margins.

## SECTION 1. Macro Context

Demand for identity intelligence, fraud detection, compliance, investigations, and risk analytics remains structurally favorable. Digital transactions, real estate safety, synthetic identity fraud, law enforcement data needs, collections, and know-your-customer workflows all support long-term demand.

Data privacy and regulation are the macro risks. Data businesses can face pressure from privacy laws, data-source restrictions, permissible-use rules, litigation, and reputational scrutiny. A strong compliance framework is essential.

AI is a mixed macro factor. AI can improve product capability, matching, automation, and user workflows. It can also increase competitive intensity if customers expect more analytics for the same price or if new entrants use AI to replicate workflows.

Small-cap software/data valuations are sensitive to rates and risk appetite. RDVT's high multiple can compress if growth decelerates or small-cap sentiment weakens.

Macro score: positive for demand, mixed for regulation and valuation.

## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

Base rate for data analytics companies: businesses with proprietary data, compliance infrastructure, and embedded workflows can compound at high margins. However, data-source dependence and privacy regulation are real risks.

Base rate for small-cap high-margin software/data companies: when revenue growth stays above 20%, premium multiples can persist; when growth falls into the mid-teens, multiples can compress quickly.

Base rate for adjusted EBITDA stories: high adjusted EBITDA margins are valuable, but investors must watch stock compensation, data costs, capex, and reinvestment needs.

Base rate for vertical SaaS-like safety tools: products such as FOREWARN can scale efficiently if user adoption is viral through associations/brokers, but churn and ARPU matter.

Base-rate conclusion: RDVT deserves a quality premium, but the current valuation demands sustained growth.

## SECTION 2. Business Quality

RDVT's quality is shown by margins. Q1 2026 adjusted gross margin of 85% [Actual] and adjusted EBITDA margin of 41% [Actual] are software-like. That indicates strong data/product operating leverage.

The company operates at a meaningful but still early scale. Q1 revenue of $25.8M [Actual] implies an annualized run rate just above $100M [Estimate]. At this scale, high margins suggest strong unit economics, but sales efficiency and total addressable market still need proof.

The balance sheet is clean. Cash was $43.5M [Actual], with no debt [Actual]. That reduces financing risk and supports repurchases. The company repurchased 73,250 shares year-to-date and had $15.6M remaining under its repurchase program [Actual/Estimate].

Growth is healthy but not explosive. Reported revenue growth of 17% [Actual] is solid, and underlying growth above 20% after excluding one-time Q1 2025 revenue is better [Actual/Estimate]. The market will need continued evidence that growth can stay near or above 20%.

Business quality score: 12/15. Margins, cash, and data economics are strong; deductions are regulatory/data risk and valuation-sensitive growth.

## SECTION 3.5. Second-Order Effects

AI reinvestment has second-order margin effects. Management expects near-term adjusted EBITDA margins to trend in the mid-to-high 30% range as it reinvests in AI and go-to-market [Actual/Estimate]. This is good long term but could reduce near-term earnings optics.

High margins have second-order valuation effects. If RDVT can grow revenue 20%+ while keeping adjusted EBITDA margins above 35%, the stock can justify a premium multiple.

Privacy regulation has second-order data-source risk. A legal or regulatory change can affect data access and model quality, not just compliance cost.

Repurchases have second-order signaling value. Buying back shares while profitable and debt-free signals confidence, but repurchases at high multiples must be monitored.
