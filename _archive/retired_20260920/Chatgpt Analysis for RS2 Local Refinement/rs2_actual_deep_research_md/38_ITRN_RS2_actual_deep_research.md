## SECTION 0. Analysis Overview

Ituran is an Israeli telematics and location-services company with a recurring subscriber base across stolen vehicle recovery, fleet management, connected-car, and usage-based insurance services. Its main markets include Israel, Brazil, and Latin America [Actual]. The attractive part of the business is recurring subscription revenue; the less attractive part is geopolitical, currency, and emerging-market exposure.

Q1 2026 was a strong quarter. Revenue reached $102.7M [Actual], up 19% year over year and above $100M quarterly revenue for the first time. Subscription revenue was $75.4M [Actual], up 21% and approximately 73% of revenue [Estimate]. Product revenue was $27.3M [Actual], up 12%. EBITDA was $26.7M [Actual], up 15%, with a 26.0% margin [Actual]. Net income was $16.8M [Actual], or $0.85 diluted EPS [Actual], up 15%.

Capital return is meaningful. The board approved a $0.50/share Q1 2026 dividend totaling about $10M [Actual]. In 2025, the company distributed $60M of dividends, including a special dividend, representing approximately 100% of net income according to company IR commentary [Actual]. Ituran also had net cash and marketable securities of about $108M at March 31, 2026 [Actual].

The valuation is no longer obviously cheap. At roughly $60.85 and market cap around $1.13B-$1.21B [Actual], ITRN trades around 17.9x Q1 annualized EPS [Estimate] and 10.5x-11.3x Q1 annualized EBITDA before net-cash adjustment [Estimate]. Cash-adjusted EV/EBITDA is closer to 9.5x-10.3x [Estimate]. That is fair for a growing recurring-revenue telematics company, but not a deep bargain.

RS2 conclusion: ITRN is a Hold / buy-on-pullback. The business is quality, recurring, cash-generative, and shareholder-friendly. New buying is best below $52-$55 or after another quarter proves subscriber growth and EBITDA margins can sustain a higher earnings base.

## SECTION 1. Macro Context

Ituran benefits from structural demand for vehicle tracking, stolen vehicle recovery, fleet optimization, insurance telematics, and connected-car services. These are practical services tied to asset protection and operating efficiency, not purely discretionary software.

The company is exposed to country and currency risk. Israel introduces geopolitical and security risk. Brazil and Latin America introduce FX, inflation, consumer-credit, vehicle-market, and regulatory volatility. A U.S. investor also faces translation risk because economic exposures are not purely U.S. dollar-based.

Vehicle sales and insurance penetration matter. New car sales, used-car activity, insurance partnerships, fleet activity, and financing conditions can influence installations and subscriber additions. In weaker auto markets, product revenue can slow before subscription revenue follows.

The macro backdrop for fleet telematics is favorable over time because logistics, security, driver behavior, insurance pricing, and asset utilization keep pushing vehicles toward connected services.

Macro score: positive for long-term connected-vehicle demand, mixed for country/FX exposure.

## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

Base rate for subscription telematics: recurring services with high installed bases can compound well when churn is low and installation pipelines remain healthy.

Base rate for emerging-market recurring businesses: growth can be strong, but reported dollars and multiples can be volatile because of currency and local macro shocks.

Base rate for high-dividend small/mid-cap foreign stocks: capital returns are attractive, but dividend levels may vary with earnings, cash needs, taxes, and board discretion.

Base rate for vehicle hardware-plus-service models: product sales can be cyclical; the subscription layer is more durable. The best underwriting focuses on subscription revenue and subscriber count rather than hardware revenue alone.

Base-rate conclusion: ITRN deserves a better multiple than a hardware vendor, but less than a U.S. pure SaaS company because of geography, FX, and vehicle-cycle exposure.

## SECTION 2. Business Quality

Ituran's quality comes from its subscriber base and recurring subscription revenue. Q1 2026 subscription revenue of $75.4M was 73% of total revenue [Actual/Estimate], giving the company high revenue visibility compared with one-time hardware businesses.

The company provides stolen vehicle recovery, fleet management, connected-car, and usage-based insurance solutions [Actual]. These services are embedded into insurers, fleets, dealers, and end-users. Once installed, the customer has a practical reason to keep paying.

Margins are healthy. Q1 2026 EBITDA margin was 26.0% [Actual], and net income margin was 16.3% [Actual]. That is strong for a business with both hardware/product and service components.

Cash generation supports dividends. Q1 operating cash flow was $18.2M [Actual], compared with net income of $16.8M [Actual]. Net cash and marketable securities of about $108M [Actual] give flexibility for dividends, buybacks, and reinvestment.

Business-quality caveat: the company operates in jurisdictions with elevated macro and FX volatility. Also, product revenue still matters for subscriber acquisition, so a vehicle-market slowdown can affect future recurring revenue.

Business quality score: 11.5/15. Recurring revenue and cash returns are attractive; country/FX and vehicle-market exposure limit the score.

## SECTION 3.5. Second-Order Effects

Subscriber growth has second-order operating leverage. Each subscriber added through product installations can create years of recurring subscription revenue, improving revenue quality and margin stability.

Dividends have second-order investor-base effects. Consistent dividends can attract income investors and support the stock, but high payout ratios can also reduce reinvestment flexibility.

Country risk has second-order multiple effects. Even if dollar earnings grow, U.S. investors may assign a lower multiple during Israel or Brazil risk-off periods.

Telematics data can create second-order product opportunities. Usage-based insurance, fleet optimization, and connected-car analytics may increase ARPU if Ituran deepens partner integrations.
