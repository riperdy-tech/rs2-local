TASK. Place this company inside the population distribution of its return on invested capital five years from now. The return on invested capital is the after-tax operating profit the company earns per dollar of capital tied up in the business (equity plus debt, less cash). The company starts from its own recent level (sections S and F show it), and over the years the level moves from there toward the typical level of the company's industry;  the distribution in section R describes where companies in a similar state stand five years on. The same level is the return the company earns on the new capital it invests to grow, so it also tells how much capital each further dollar of growth needs. A company whose advantages are durable, or whose business needs little capital for each dollar of added revenue, sits high; a company whose advantages are eroding, or that must reinvest heavily for a small return, sits low. Judge it from its own record and business model. The distribution is in section R; it describes companies in a similar state to this one (section S). Use the filed figures (F), the company's own documents (D) and the company's state (S).

HOW TO ANSWER
- "percentile": a number from 5 to 95. 50 is the typical company in a similar state; 80 means you expect this company to be above 80 percent of them. Do not default to 50: put the company where its evidence puts it, and no further.
- A percentile below 10 or above 90 needs hard evidence: at least one citation from section D or Q whose quote holds a firm commitment (a contract, an agreement, backlog, an award, a purchase order) or a figure together with company guidance, capacity or a stated plan. A general outlook sentence is not hard evidence.
- "width": narrow, normal or wide. How sure you are of the position.
- "reasons": one to six short statements of why the company belongs there, drawing on its capital spending, working capital and business model.
- "citations": one to six objects {"section": "F" or "D" or "S", "quote": text}. Each quote is 12 to 600 characters of contiguous text copied exactly from that section (no ellipsis, no paraphrase, no changes to numbers or punctuation).
