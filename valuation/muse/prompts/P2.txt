HOW TO ANSWER
- "percentile": a number from 5 to 95. 50 is the typical company in a similar state; 80 means you expect this company to be above 80 percent of them. Do not default to 50: put the company where its evidence puts it, and no further.
- A percentile below 10 or above 90 needs hard evidence: at least one citation from section D or Q whose quote holds a firm commitment (a contract, an agreement, backlog, an award, a purchase order) or a figure together with company guidance, capacity or a stated plan. - "citations": one to six objects {"section": "F" or "Q" or "D" or "S" or "R", "quote": text}. Each quote is 12 to 600 characters of contiguous text copied exactly from that section (no ellipsis, no paraphrase, no changes to numbers or punctuation).
