## SECTION 0. Analysis Overview

Halozyme is a high-margin biopharma royalty and drug-delivery platform built around ENHANZE, a recombinant human hyaluronidase technology that enables subcutaneous delivery of injected biologics. The company has expanded beyond ENHANZE through Hypercon and Surf Bio hyperconcentration technologies, aiming to extend royalty opportunities into the 2030s and mid-2040s.

Q1 2026 was strong. Total revenue increased 42% year over year to $376.7 million, royalty revenue increased 43% to $240.7 million, operating income rose to $184.5 million, net income was $150.0 million, adjusted EBITDA was $229.5 million, and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $1.60. Management reiterated 2026 guidance for $1.710-$1.810 billion of total revenue, $1.130-$1.170 billion of royalty revenue, $1.125-$1.205 billion of adjusted EBITDA, and $7.75-$8.25 non-GAAP diluted EPS.

At a roughly $9.5-$9.8 billion market cap and roughly $11 billion enterprise value after debt/cash adjustments, Halozyme trades around 9.5x-10x FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance and under 10x midpoint non-GAAP EPS. That is cheap if royalty growth and duration are durable.

The market's concern is not current growth. The concern is the duration and defensibility of future royalties, dependency on partner products, patent/litigation outcomes, concentration in several blockbuster partnered products, and elevated debt/convertible-note complexity after acquisitions and refinancing.

RS2 summary: HALO is a Buy. The valuation is too low for the scale of 2026 cash generation and royalty growth if management's duration case is even mostly right. Position size should still respect patent, litigation, and partner concentration risk.

## SECTION 1. Macro Context

The macro trend favors subcutaneous drug delivery. Biologic therapies are growing, patients prefer lower treatment burden, providers want shorter infusion-chair time, and payers can benefit from care-site flexibility when economics are aligned. Halozyme's technology can convert certain IV therapies into faster subcutaneous administration.

The healthcare-policy backdrop is mixed. Subcutaneous products can improve patient experience, but drug pricing reform and Medicare negotiation can affect partner-product economics. Halozyme's investor site includes IRA commentary, highlighting that policy treatment of subcutaneous versus intravenous products is strategically important.

Biopharma partnership demand is strong. In 2026, Halozyme announced new agreements with Vertex, Oruka, and GSK, including GSK oncology targets and Hypercon agreements. That indicates continued partner appetite for delivery platforms.

Macro risk: if payers or regulators treat subcutaneous conversions as insufficiently differentiated, royalty value and partner willingness could suffer. Conversely, if at-home and lower-burden administration becomes more valued, Halozyme's relevance increases.

Macro score: favorable long-term adoption environment, with policy and patent friction.

## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

Base rate for royalty platforms: when protected by strong IP and tied to blockbuster products, royalty platforms can be exceptional businesses with high incremental margins and strong free cash flow. When IP duration is questioned, multiples compress quickly.

Base rate for drug-delivery technology: partner validation matters more than platform claims. Halozyme has validation from Janssen, argenx, Roche, GSK, Vertex, Takeda, Pfizer, and others.

Base rate for single-platform risk: concentration in one technology can create cliff risk. Halozyme's acquisitions of Hypercon and Surf Bio are strategically sensible because they diversify future platform life.

Base rate for low-P/E biopharma royalty stories: the market is often discounting either patent cliffs, concentration, or one-time economics. In HALO's case, royalty duration is the central issue.

Base-rate conclusion: attractive if duration is real. The right question is not "is 2026 cheap?" but "how much of this royalty stream persists into the 2030s?"

## SECTION 2. Business Quality

Halozyme's ENHANZE technology uses rHuPH20 to temporarily reduce the subcutaneous-space barrier and enable rapid delivery of high-volume injectable drugs. The company licenses the technology to partners, who develop and commercialize products. Halozyme receives upfronts, milestones, and royalties.

The platform has commercial proof. ENHANZE has touched more than one million patient lives through ten commercialized products across more than 100 markets. Q1 2026 royalty growth was driven primarily by DARZALEX SC, VYVGART Hytrulo, Phesgo, and other recently launched products.

Partner breadth is excellent. Halozyme lists partners including Roche, Takeda, Pfizer, Janssen, AbbVie, Eli Lilly, Bristol Myers Squibb, argenx, ViiV Healthcare, Chugai, Acumen, Merus, Skye, GSK, Vertex, Oruka, and others. This reduces single-partner risk, though current royalty dollars remain concentrated.

The business model has strong operating leverage. Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA margin was about 61%, and full-year 2026 guidance implies adjusted EBITDA margin around 66% at the midpoint.

Growth runway is visible. Management expects royalty revenue above $1 billion in 2026 and total revenue above $2 billion by 2028. It also expects 13 ENHANZE products in development by year-end 2026 and sees Hypercon product approvals beginning around 2030/2031.

Business quality score: 13/15. Halozyme has a validated, high-margin, partner-leveraged model. The deduction is for IP duration and concentration risk.

## SECTION 3. Valuation

Reference valuation inputs:

- Share price: about $77-$78.
- Market capitalization: about $9.5-$9.8 billion.
- Enterprise value: roughly $11 billion to $11.5 billion, depending on debt/cash source.
- FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance midpoint: $1.165 billion.
- FY2026 non-GAAP EPS guidance midpoint: $8.00.

P/E: $78 divided by $8.00 midpoint non-GAAP EPS is 9.8x. For a company guiding 22%-30% revenue growth and 71%-83% adjusted EBITDA growth, that is cheap.

EV/EBITDA: $11.3 billion EV divided by $1.165 billion adjusted EBITDA midpoint is about 9.7x. That is attractive for a high-margin royalty growth platform, assuming the royalty stream has duration.

Buyback yield: management expects to repurchase at least $400 million in 2026. Against a market cap near $9.6 billion, that is over 4% gross buyback yield.

Valuation conclusion: undervalued on 2026 numbers. The market is assigning a substantial royalty-duration and patent-risk discount. The discount appears too large given partner momentum and new platform deals.

## SECTION 3.5. Second-Order Effects

Buybacks can materially increase per-share value because Halozyme is repurchasing stock at a low multiple of guided earnings. If the royalty stream lasts, buybacks are highly accretive.

New platform deals can change terminal value. ENHANZE drives current royalties, but Hypercon and Surf Bio can extend the story beyond the current patent debate. Vertex, Oruka, and other Hypercon deals are important because they signal partner validation outside ENHANZE.

Partner product label expansions compound royalties. DARZALEX Faspro new indications and VYVGART Hytrulo broader approval expand addressable patient pools without Halozyme needing to run those commercial engines.

Patent litigation has asymmetric second-order impact. Positive outcomes can unlock multiple expansion; negative outcomes can compress the stock even if near-term earnings remain strong.

## SECTION 4. Scenarios

Bull case:

2026 guidance is met or raised, royalty growth continues above 25%, litigation/IP outcomes support duration, new ENHANZE and Hypercon programs expand the 2030s pipeline, and buybacks reduce share count materially. The stock rerates to 13x-15x EPS, implying $105-$120.

Base case:

Halozyme meets FY2026 guidance, royalty revenue exceeds $1.1 billion, buybacks are executed, but the market keeps a royalty-duration discount. Stock compounds through EPS growth and repurchases toward $85-$95.

Bear case:

Patent/litigation concerns intensify, partner-product growth slows, debt concerns rise, and the market values HALO at 7x-8x EPS. Downside could be $55-$65.

Stress case:

Adverse patent rulings or policy changes impair post-2027 royalty confidence while debt remains high. The stock could trade below $50 despite near-term profitability.

## SECTION 4.5. Horizon Arbitrage

The near-term market is focused on patent duration and royalty cliffs. The longer-term question is whether Halozyme can use current cash generation to repurchase shares and bridge to new waves of ENHANZE, Hypercon, and Surf Bio royalty streams.

If the company has enough royalty duration through the late 2020s and new platform launches in the early 2030s, today's low multiple is an opportunity. If the bridge fails, the low multiple is a value trap.

Horizon conclusion: favorable for investors willing to underwrite IP risk and monitor pipeline transitions closely.

## SECTION 5.5. Behavioral

Bullish mistake: assuming low P/E means no risk. HALO is cheap because investors are discounting real IP and duration questions.

Bearish mistake: assuming all royalty streams end abruptly. Partner label expansions, new launches, and platform diversification can extend cash flows longer than skeptics model.

Anchoring mistake: focusing only on ENHANZE patent debates and ignoring Hypercon/Surf Bio deals.

Behavioral rule: tie position size to evidence of royalty duration, not just current-year EPS.

## SECTION 9. Red Team

Bear argument: HALO is cheap because the market sees a royalty cliff. ENHANZE's current royalty streams may fade faster than management suggests, partner products may face competition or pricing pressure, and debt/convertibles reduce equity quality. Non-GAAP EPS may overstate durable earnings.

Bull rebuttal: Current royalty growth is above 40%, 2026 guidance is very strong, buybacks are large, and new Hypercon/ENHANZE agreements indicate partners still value Halozyme's delivery platforms. Even a discounted terminal value can justify a higher stock price if cash generation persists through the late 2020s.

Red-team verdict: the bear case is real but over-discounted at under 10x guided EPS.
