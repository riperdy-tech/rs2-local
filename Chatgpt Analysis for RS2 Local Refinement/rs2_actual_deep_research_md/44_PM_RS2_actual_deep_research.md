## SECTION 0. Analysis Overview

Philip Morris International is the global tobacco company furthest along in the transition from combustibles to smoke-free nicotine. The core thesis is that IQOS heated tobacco and ZYN nicotine pouches allow PM to grow earnings while reducing dependence on cigarettes. This distinguishes PM from more combustible-dependent tobacco peers.

Q1 2026 performance exceeded management's expectations. PMI cited outstanding IQOS delivery and 16% adjusted diluted EPS growth in Q1 [Actual]. Smoke-free products are the strategic core: in 2025, smoke-free products accounted for 41.5% of total net revenues and nearly 43% of gross profit [Actual]. Smoke-free shipment volumes grew 12.8% in 2025, smoke-free net revenues grew 15.0%, and smoke-free gross profit grew 20.3% [Actual].

ZYN is a mixed but positive story. Q1 2026 had weaker U.S. ZYN shipment volume due to prior-year comparisons and inventory dynamics, with reports noting shipments down nearly 24% [Actual/Reported]. However, retail demand remained stronger, and FDA approval of a modified risk label for ZYN in mid-2026 is a potential demand and positioning catalyst [Actual/Reported].

The valuation is no longer tobacco-deep-value. PM has rerated because investors recognize IQOS and ZYN as growth assets. Dividend yield is around 3.25% [Actual/Estimate], with a quarterly dividend of $1.47 [Actual]. That is attractive but lower than classic tobacco yield levels.

RS2 conclusion: PM is a Buy / staged accumulate. It is the highest-quality tobacco transition story, with real smoke-free scale and global execution. The correct position size should reflect regulatory, nicotine-policy, FX, and valuation risk.

## SECTION 1. Macro Context

Global cigarette demand continues to decline structurally, but nicotine demand is shifting rather than disappearing. PM benefits from that shift because IQOS and ZYN are leading reduced-risk or smoke-free alternatives in multiple markets.

Regulation is central. Tobacco and nicotine products remain heavily regulated worldwide. However, PM's smoke-free strategy can benefit when regulators differentiate between combusted cigarettes and lower-risk alternatives. The FDA's modified-risk authorization for ZYN-related claims is strategically favorable [Actual/Reported].

FX matters. PM reports in U.S. dollars but earns globally. Currency movements can meaningfully affect reported revenue, EPS, debt translation, and dividend coverage.

U.S. ZYN is a large opportunity but not frictionless. Capacity, FDA authorizations, flavor rules, modified-risk claims, competition from Rogue/Velo/on!/others, and inventory normalization all matter.

Macro score: positive for smoke-free transition, negative for cigarette volumes and regulatory unpredictability.

## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

Base rate for tobacco companies: strong pricing power and dividends can generate returns despite volume decline. But long-term multiple expansion requires a credible reduced-risk growth engine.

Base rate for category transitions: incumbents can fail when new products cannibalize high-margin legacy products. PM is ahead of this base rate because smoke-free is already 41.5% of 2025 net revenue [Actual].

Base rate for nicotine pouch growth: early growth can be explosive, but competition, flavors, regulation, and inventory timing cause volatility.

Base rate for high-quality consumer staples at premium multiples: quality can be worth paying for, but entry price matters when growth expectations are embedded.

Base-rate conclusion: PM is the best-in-class tobacco transition name, deserving a premium, but not immune to regulatory and valuation risk.

## SECTION 2. Business Quality

PM's business quality is high because it combines global tobacco cash flows, strong brands, pricing power, and real smoke-free growth. IQOS is the centerpiece: heated tobacco can reduce combustible exposure while preserving nicotine economics and device ecosystem advantages.

Smoke-free scale is now large. In 2025, smoke-free products accounted for 41.5% of total net revenues and nearly 43% of gross profit [Actual]. That is no longer a speculative narrative.

IQOS continues to gain share. Q1 2026 investor materials highlighted IQOS/ZYN/VEEV shipment growth and record IQOS share in Japan, with PMI heated tobacco unit adjusted share in Japan around 34.9% and heat-not-burn category share around 53% in the top convenience chains cited [Actual].

ZYN adds U.S. growth optionality. Despite shipment volatility, FDA modified-risk labeling can strengthen ZYN's adult-smoker conversion message and category legitimacy [Actual/Reported].

Business-quality caveat: nicotine regulation can change abruptly. Also, ZYN's Q1 shipment decline shows that category growth can be obscured by supply, channel inventory, and competitive dynamics.

Business quality score: 13/15. PM is a top-tier consumer/nicotine transition asset; deductions are regulation, FX, and leverage/dividend constraints.

## SECTION 3.5. Second-Order Effects

Smoke-free mix has second-order multiple expansion. As smoke-free crosses 50% of net revenue and gross profit, PM may trade less like legacy tobacco and more like a nicotine growth platform.

ZYN modified-risk labeling has second-order category effects. It can improve adult-smoker conversion messaging, retailer confidence, and regulatory legitimacy.

IQOS scale has second-order ecosystem effects. Device adoption can create switching costs and repeat consumable revenue.

FX has second-order dividend effects. A strong dollar can pressure reported EPS and dividend coverage even if local-market performance is strong.

## SECTION 4. Scenarios

Bull case:

IQOS continues strong share gains, ZYN recovers sharply in H2 2026, smoke-free exceeds 50% of revenue, adjusted EPS grows high single digits to low double digits, and PM trades at a premium staples multiple. Fair value: $210-$230 [Assumption].

Base case:

Smoke-free grows low-to-mid teens, cigarettes decline gradually, EPS grows mid-to-high single digits, and dividend grows modestly. Fair value: $175-$200 [Assumption].

Bear case:

ZYN competition/regulation limits growth, IQOS slows, FX pressures EPS, and tobacco multiples compress. Fair value: $135-$155 [Assumption].

Stress case:

Major nicotine regulation, tax shock, or smoke-free safety controversy impairs growth. Downside below $120 [Assumption].

## SECTION 4.5. Horizon Arbitrage

PM's horizon edge is that many investors still group it with combustible tobacco, while the business mix is rapidly becoming smoke-free. If smoke-free reaches majority scale, the market may continue rewarding PM.

The risk is that the market already knows this and has rerated the stock. The remaining edge is execution, not discovery.

Horizon conclusion: favorable, especially versus legacy tobacco peers.
