At $364.20, ROP trades at roughly 16.6x the midpoint of updated 2026 adjusted DEPS guidance [Estimate]. That is unusually reasonable for a high-quality recurring-revenue compounder.

Core view: Buy, with normal execution risk. ROP screens better after the price reset than many other quality growth names.

## SECTION 1. Macro Context

Roper's portfolio is less economically sensitive than traditional industrials because much of the business is software, workflow, data, and mission-critical vertical applications. However, end-market demand still matters, and acquisition multiples are influenced by rates and credit conditions.

Higher rates can reduce the value of future cash flows and make acquisitions harder to underwrite. Conversely, if private-market valuations reset, ROP's capital allocation engine can improve. Management's stated deployable capacity of more than $5 billion gives the company flexibility [Actual].

Macro conclusion: above-average resilience with acquisition-cycle optionality.

The base rate for serial acquirers is mixed. Many destroy value by overpaying. Roper's base rate is better because it has long emphasized asset-light, high-margin, cash-generative niche businesses and free-cash-flow-per-share compounding.

Base-rate score: positive.
