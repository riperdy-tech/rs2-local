## SECTION 0. Analysis Overview

Autodesk is a mission-critical design and engineering software company serving architecture, engineering, construction, manufacturing, media, and infrastructure workflows. Fiscal Q1 2027 results were strong: billings were $1.688 billion, up 18%; revenue was $1.934 billion, up 18%; GAAP operating margin was 28%; non-GAAP operating margin was 39%; GAAP EPS was $2.32; non-GAAP EPS was $2.99; operating cash flow was $893 million; and free cash flow was $876 million, up 58% [Actual].

At $207.48, ADSK has a $43.99 billion market cap, P/E of 30.3x, and EPS of $6.85 [Actual]. On annualized Q1 non-GAAP EPS of $11.96, the multiple is about 17.3x [Estimate], though seasonality means annualization is imperfect. For high-margin mission-critical software growing revenue 18%, that is attractive.

Core view: Buy. ADSK appears mispriced relative to software quality and Q1 momentum.

## SECTION 1. Macro Context

Autodesk is exposed to construction, infrastructure, manufacturing, media, and design spending. Macro cyclicality exists, especially in commercial construction and manufacturing, but subscription software and mission-critical workflows make revenue more durable than project-based services.

Data-center, infrastructure, and AI-related design demand are supportive. Rates affect construction activity and software multiples.

Macro conclusion: some cyclical exposure, but subscription software model is resilient.

## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

The base rate for mission-critical vertical software with high renewal rates and pricing power is strong. Autodesk has deep workflow embedding and a large installed base.

The base-rate risk is that mature software companies can disappoint if billings slow or customers push back on pricing. Q1 billings and revenue growth of 18% reduce that concern [Actual].

Base-rate score: positive.

## SECTION 2. Business Quality

Autodesk's quality is high because its tools sit inside professional workflows where switching costs are meaningful. Non-GAAP operating margin of 39% and Q1 free cash flow of $876 million show strong software economics [Actual].

Quality score: 8.5/10. Mission-critical software, high margin, good growth.
