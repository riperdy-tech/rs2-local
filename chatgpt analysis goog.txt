## SECTION 1.5. Base Rate Check

For a mega-cap quality compounder with net cash, monopoly-like distribution, and positive FCF, the 5-year survival base rate is effectively **>95%** [Estimate]. The more relevant base rate is not survival; it is whether the current AI capex cycle earns ROIC above WACC.

**Base-rate risk:** large-scale tech infrastructure cycles often look attractive early, but market returns depend on whether incremental demand absorbs capacity without margin compression. Alphabet’s company-specific reasons to deviate positively are: first-party Search distribution, Cloud backlog, TPU/custom silicon economics, massive internal AI use cases, and existing cash generation.

**Shock check:** My base case assumes Alphabet eventually earns above-WACC returns on AI capex, but not immediately. This is not a 2× base-rate optimism case because the DCF explicitly compresses 2026–2027 FCF.
