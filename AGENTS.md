# RS2 LOCAL AGENTS CORE DIRECTIVE: INSTITUTIONAL VALUATION ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEMIC INTEGRITY

This document governs all prompt engineering, valuation logic, research synthesis, and execution models inside `RS2 Local`.

---

## 1. FIRST PRINCIPLE: SYSTEMIC INTEGRITY OVER BAND-AIDS
* **No Narrow Heuristic Patches**: When an analytical flaw or counter-example is uncovered, NEVER implement superficial, narrow prompt tweaks designed only to fix that specific company or sector.
* **Solve the Systemic Root Cause**: Identify the overarching methodological defect (e.g. flawed accounting normalization, lack of installed-base modeling, rigid multiple caps, unrealistic bear-case distributions) and fix the valuation architecture universally across all archetypes.

---

## 2. VALUATION METHODOLOGY: COMPLETE ECONOMIC MACHINERY
* **Beyond Backward-Looking Accounting**: Accounting statements record past cash and accruals. Economic value is determined by future sustainable cash generation and competitive advantage.
* **Installed-Base & Lifecycle Monetization**:
  * For businesses where upfront bookings/sales drive multi-decade aftermarket services, spare parts, software subscriptions, or maintenance contracts (e.g. power generation, aerospace, semiconductors, medical systems):
    * Customer advance commitments and contract liabilities must be recognized as leading indicators of installed-base expansion.
    * The multi-decade, high-margin aftermarket annuity stream must be modeled explicitly into terminal earnings power, not erased by simplistic working-capital deductions.
* **Avoid Terminal Multiple Dogmatism**:
  * Classical single-stage Gordon Growth models ($TV = FCF / (WACC - g)$) with static GDP growth caps ($g \le 3.5\%$) mechanically force exit multiples to 15x–17x FCF.
  * In a capital-abundant, inflationary macro environment with structural physical bottlenecks, pricing a mission-critical compounder at a commodity liquidation multiple creates a systemic bias: it rejects elite compounders and favors decaying value traps.
  * Multiples must reflect competitive durability, secular growth drivers, and prevailing macroeconomic regime realities.

---

## 3. SCENARIO DISPERSION & REALISTIC RISK BOUNDS
* **Bear Case Integrity**: A Bear scenario must represent a severe, plausible cyclical downturn or operational misstep. It must NOT be a mathematical absurdity (e.g. projecting an industry leader with a multi-year contracted backlog to experience complete margin collapse to near-zero).
* **Cross-Check Against Institutional Reality**: If the model's Bear case is wildly disconnected from the most pessimistic institutional bears in the market, inspect the scenario assumptions for unrealistic catastrophic bias.
